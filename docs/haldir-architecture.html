Source-informed architecture
Native mandate route
Read this as a logical architecture, not a deployment claim. Components are present in source. Full public wallet → payment → delivery → recovery acceptance is not established by this artifact. The browser demo remains a separate scripted simulation.

On narrow screens, scroll the map horizontally. The backend boxes are modules and adapters, not independently deployed microservices or independent custodians.

Cheap does not mean allowed.

The memorable case is a small payment that fits the budget but targets an excluded recipient. Recipient permission and available principal are independent checks.

A receipt is not a report.

Transfer confirmation, purchased content and a useful evidence-linked report are separate outcomes. An unavailable source or unknown payment stays visible.

Recovery keeps its identity.

Reconcile the same job, order and attempt. Do not issue a fresh payment because a response was lost, or call available funds recovered before confirmation.
